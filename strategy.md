# Digital Susu — partnership pack

This file is the material that is not the product. The app is the product. Nothing here is a signed contract.

The live app lets a customer choose the savings amount. The GH₵ 600 case below is the illustrated bank story, not a fixed product rule.

## Which door to use

| Path | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Partner with a licensed bank or savings and loans | Use this. They hold the savings and the licence. We keep the app. |
| Apply to the Bank of Ghana alone | Too slow and too expensive for a first launch. |
| Partner with another lender that already has a credit builder | Do not. They will treat the product as theirs. |

Opening line: we manufacture customers the bank can trust. They hold the savings. We run the app, the market, and the support. If someone stops, the box closes and nobody is left owing.

## Illustrated case

The bank story uses a GH₵ 600 box, a GH₵ 50 month, and a small out-of-pocket piece, reported as a secured facility. At 100,000 boxes that is GH₵ 60 million of locked savings. A 24% treasury illustration is about GH₵ 14.4 million gross, before fees. Those figures are a pitch, not a forecast we have earned. Treasury-bill activity stays off in the app until a securities registration exists.

Fee illustration, if the bank wants one: GH₵ 15 once, and GH₵ 1 a month inside the drop. The app currently charges only the activation fee the super admin sets. A second monthly fee is not shown to the customer.

## Letter of intent (template)

To the Head of Retail, [Bank].

We propose a custody partnership for Digital Susu. The customer chooses a savings amount. It is pushed to the bank and twelve months are marked from it. The bank files the credit record. We run the app, USSD *385#, the market agents, and first-line support. The bank holds the money, the ledger on its core, and the licence. A first pilot is capped, region-locked, and filed together under the Bank of Ghana sandbox. This letter is not a contract.

## What each side does after a yes

- Technology: wallet pulls, the month marker, USSD, and the webhook that refuses a duplicate.
- Risk: 72-hour retry, 15-day grace, then set-off. A closed box is not a bad debt.
- Market: Makola and Kejetia first, then the flyer and the *385# line.
- Compliance: key facts before signup, unbundled consent, Data Protection Act 843, no chats or photos.

## Key facts we already show in the app

The savings stay locked for 12 months. The activation fee is once. A missed month retries for 72 hours, then grace, then a clean close. The customer cannot spend the savings early. Complaints go to our desk, then the bank, then the Bank of Ghana.

## Pilot gates

Do not widen the book until all of these hold:

- Webhook pulls succeed on at least 99.7% of attempts, including the retry.
- Every set-off leaves a zero lock. No manual repair.
- No savings balance goes below zero.
- The cohort stays inside the cap.
- The customer understood the flyer. If they did not, fix the words before spending more.

## Flyer

Do susu for a year and build your credit. Choose what you save. The bank keeps it. Twelve months later it comes back, and the bank has the record. You need a Ghana Card and a mobile money wallet. Dial *385# or ask an agent at the market.

## Town-hall hook

Ameeeooo. The banks ask for a payslip you do not have. This one does not. You choose what to save. It stays at a licensed bank. If the market is slow and you stop, nobody comes to the shop, and you do not owe a pesewa. If you finish the year, the savings come back.

## Radio hook

Sister, the rain stopped the market. The old lender will come for the stock. This one is different. Dial *385#. The savings are locked at the bank. If you cannot continue, the box closes and you owe nothing.

## Desk scripts

Missed month: you are safe. We retry for 72 hours, then grace is 15 days. Your name is not marked late yet.

Box closed: the savings cleared it. You owe nothing. The remainder is back in the wallet.

SMS arrived and the ring did not move: the money is safe. We match the reference and we do not take it twice.

## What we will not say

We will not say the customer is buying a treasury bill. We will not say a return is guaranteed. We will not say we file the credit bureau ourselves. The bank does that. Those switches exist in the app and stay off.
